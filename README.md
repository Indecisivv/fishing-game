# Fishin' For Love
*Fill your need, find love*

"In a city where some people have water creatures for heads, you hop on a dating app to fill the need you feel."

Sometimes, you feel a different hunger that cannot be satiated and you must explore other options. Thankfully, a new app called 'Hook, Line, Sinker' offers you a way to do that! Promoting itself with the simple tagline, 'Find your one in a seallion', the app offers you a way to connect with beautiful singles near you. Use this fun new app to fish for the stranger who will fulfill that hunger you couldn't possibly bring yourself to describe!

Created in a week for the [Gone Fishing Game Jam II](https://itch.io/jam/gone-fishing-game-jam-ii)! 




## Features

- 🐟︎ Three romance paths
- 🐟︎ Four possible endings
- 🐟︎ 6k Words
- 🐟︎ 6 Original Artwork CGs!
